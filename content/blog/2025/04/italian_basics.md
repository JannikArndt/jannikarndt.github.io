After six weeks in Palermo, these are the words and phrases I use almost every day. So if you're preparing for a trip to Italy, this is a good start!

<!--more-->

## Basics


| Emoji | Italiano | Deutsch | English |
|:-----:|:--------|:--------|:--------|
| 👋 | **Ciao!** | Hallo! | Hi / Bye! |
| 🙏 | **Grazie!** | Danke! | Thank you! |
| 🙏 | **Prego!** | Bitte! | You're welcome! / Please! |
| 👍 / 👎 | **Sì / No** | Ja / Nein | Yes / No |
| 🙇 | **Scusi!** (Scusa for friends) | Entschuldigung! | Excuse me! |
| 😬 | **Mi dispiace** | Es tut mir leid | I'm sorry |
| 🫸 | **Aspetta!** | Warte! | Wait! |
| ⏳ | **Un attimo!** | Einen Moment! | Just a moment! |
| 🙌 | **Ecco!** | Bitte schön! / Hier ist es! | Here you go! / There it is! |
| 👌 | **Va bene!** | Alles klar! / In Ordnung! | Alright / Okay! |
| 🚶‍♂️ | **Andiamo!** | Los geht's! | Let's go! |


## Talking

| Emoji | Italiano | Deutsch | English |
|:-----:|:--------|:--------|:--------|
| 🤷‍♂️ | **Non ho capito.** | Ich habe es nicht verstanden. | I didn't understand. |
| 🇬🇧 | **Parli inglese?** (formal: Parla inglese?) | Sprechen Sie Englisch? | Do you speak English? |
| 🆘 | **Può aiutarmi?** | Können Sie mir helfen? | Can you help me? |
| 🇩🇪 | **Sono tedesco.** | Ich bin Deutscher. | I am German. |
| 🚻 | **Dov'è il bagno?** | Wo ist die Toilette? | Where is the bathroom? |
| 👨 | **Mi chiamo Jannik.** | Ich heiße Jannik. | My name is Jannik. |
| 🤝 | **Piacere.** | Freut mich. | Nice to meet you. |
| 👋 | **Ci vediamo!** | Bis bald! | See you! |

## Shopping

| Emoji | Italiano | Deutsch | English |
|:-----:|:--------|:--------|:--------|
| 🔍 | **Dove posso trovare ...? / Ce l'ha ...?** | Wo finde ich ...? / Haben Sie ...? | Where can I find ...? / Do you have ...? |
| 👉 | **Vorrei questo, per favore.** | Ich möchte das bitte. | I'd like this, please. |
| ☕ | **Un caffè, per favore.** | Einen Kaffee, bitte. | A coffee, please. |
| 🔓 / 🔒 | **Aperto / Chiuso** | Offen / Geschlossen | Open / Closed |
| 💰 | **Quanto costa?** | Wie viel kostet das? | How much does it cost? |
| 💸 | **Vorrei pagare, per favore.** | Ich möchte bitte bezahlen. | I'd like to pay, please. |
| 💳 | **Con carta** | Mit Karte | By card |
| 🧾 | **Lo scontrino?** | Der Kassenbon? | The receipt? |
| 👀 | **Sto solo guardando.** | Ich schaue mich nur um. | I'm just looking. |
